THE ARTWORK

Where winter becomes a revelation.

Epiphaneia is a meditation on the moment when silence reveals what noise conceals. Between frozen mountains, dark water, and the first warmth of sunlight, the landscape seems to pause at the threshold of awakening.

The snow carries stillness, the mountains hold endurance, and the reflected light suggests that even in the coldest season, something luminous can return. The work is about revelation—not a sudden answer, but the quiet recognition that beauty, hope, and clarity were present all along.

“Some truths arrive softly—like light touching a winter lake.”

THE MEANING

A name for the moment of appearing.

The title Epiphaneia evokes manifestation, appearance, and revelation. Here, rising light does not conquer winter—it coexists with it.
